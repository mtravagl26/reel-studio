# Example Studio's reel brand

> Added by By Mel Ross. A made-up brand, here to show a second setup that is very different from `brand.example.md`: fast, loud and busy where that one is calm. Nothing in it describes a real business.

## Account
Short how-to reels for people who run a small shop · owners who want one useful thing in under a minute

## Colours
- Main: #1F2A44 (navy: titles, label text)
- Pop: #F2A541 (orange: the key word, the last line of a title)
- Light: #FFF6E9 (cream: cards, backgrounds)
- Never: pastel pink, gradients

## Look
The `bold` theme: heavy caps titles, each caption word in a white box, numbers in orange.

## Fonts
- Headlines: Anton, all caps
- Captions: Inter, extra bold
- Accents: Lora Italic, for one quiet word in a title
- All three are free (SIL Open Font License) and ship in `skills/3-editing/reel-editor/themes/_fonts/`.

## Captions
One word at a time, mid-chest, each in a white box. The key word of a line gets an orange box.

## Graphics
A graphic on most lines: a stat card for any number, icon tiles for a list of 3, a label for each step.

## Sound
A pop on each graphic and a whoosh on the title. Nothing under the voice.

## Pacing
1.05x, every filler cut, a cut or zoom every 2 to 3 seconds, under 45 seconds.

## Call to action
"Save this for your next slow afternoon", always the last line.

## Rules
- Every number said out loud goes on screen
- No more than 6 words in a title
- Captions never cover the mouth
