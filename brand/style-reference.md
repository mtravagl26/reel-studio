# Style reference: the "one page" explainer

The reel this brand is tuned to, described in words so you can aim for the same feel. No footage, audio or captions from it are included here.

## The shape
- A talking-head explainer, filmed vertically on a phone, about two minutes long. One person talking straight to camera, one setting, no b-roll.
- Natural pace: normal speed, with clear filler words and long pauses taken out, so it feels like a calm conversation rather than a jump-cut sprint.
- No music and no sound effects. The voice carries it.
- The call to action is said on camera near the end, with a small pill on screen that matches what's said.

## The opening
- A two-line hook title in the serif head font (Lora), deep teal with a cream outline, sits above the head and wipes in from left to right in the first half second, the second line a beat after the first.

## Captions
- Short phrases of about three words, sentence case, white Inter semibold, placed below the chin.
- One key word per sentence in light sage, with a soft deep-teal shadow so it reads over a light top.
- No boxes, no outlines, no word-by-word bouncing.

## Camera movement
- A gentle zoom about every 6-7 seconds, landing on the start of a sentence: a slow push to 1.04x over 2.5 seconds, eased in and out, then easing back to 1.0x at the next point. You feel it more than you see it.

## Graphics
- A custom animated layer kept in the band above the head (roughly the top third of the frame), so the face and captions stay clear.
- Each graphic only shows what's being said at that moment, then leaves: a card that ticks items off as they're named, simple app icons when a tool is named, a mock document page with an arrow drawing to a to-do list, small "report" cards stacking up, scattered icons gathering into one place, a short homework checklist.
- Motion vocabulary: things enter with a soft blur-to-sharp fade and a slight scale-up (about 0.4 s), leave with a quick blur fade (about 0.3 s), get a small pulse on emphasis, and check marks and arrows draw themselves in along their path.
- Colours stay in the brand palette: deep teal, sage and cream, with darker sage, slate blue and sand as supporting tones.

## The cover
- A smiling still from the start of the clip with a short topic title in the head font, clear of the face.
