# Reel brand: By Mel Ross

This is the example brand that ships with Reel Studio. Copy it to `~/reels/brand.md` (and `brand.example.json` to `~/reels/brand.json`), then change it to fit you. The reel editor reads both files before every edit; this one is for people, the JSON is for the scripts. Keep them in step.

## Account
Practical AI and operations for small-business owners and solo founders. Audience: business owners who want AI to save them time without losing their own voice.

## Style reference
See `style-reference.md`: the reel this brand is tuned to, described in words.

## Colours
- Main: #0d2b2d deep teal (hook title text, colour blocks)
- Pop: #B9C8A5 light sage (caption highlight on key words)
- Light: #f5f2ec cream (backgrounds, outline on the hook title)
- Supporting: sage #7c8a6a, slate blue #375d6e, sand #d8c8b0, charcoal #232333
- Never: neon or loud, flashy colours

## Fonts
- Headlines: Lora, semibold
- Body and captions: Inter, semibold
- Accent: Lora Italic
- All three are free (SIL Open Font License) and ship in `brand/fonts/`. Copy them to `~/reels/fonts/`.

## Captions
Short phrases (2-4 words), sentence case, white, at mid-chest (move them below the chin when the face sits low in frame). One key word per sentence in light sage #B9C8A5, always with a soft deep-teal shadow behind it so it reads over light clothes. No boxes, no outlines on captions.

## Graphics
Clean, warm, not flashy. Minimal by default: captions plus a hook title. Anything more only shows what's being said at that moment and lives in the space above the head. No b-roll, no stock footage. The hook title is the only text with an outline. No carousel.

## Sound
No background music. No sound effects.

## Pacing
Natural: normal speed; drop clear "um"s and "uh"s and pauses over about 0.6 s. Talking-head pieces can run to about two minutes when the content earns it.

## Zooms
Subtle and gradual: a slow push to about 1.04x over 2.5 s, eased in and out, then easing back out at the next point, about every 6 s. Never a hard punch.

## Cover
Every reel gets a cover: 1080x1920 JPG, a smiling still from the start or end of the clip, plus a short topic title (2-3 lines of a few words) in the head font, in the area the profile grid shows.

## Delivery
Three files go back into the folder the footage came from: the full-quality MP4, a phone-size MP4 and the cover. Nothing is posted or scheduled automatically; posting stays a human job.

## Call to action
"Follow @bymelross for more", at the end, said on camera.

## Rules
- No b-roll.
- Zoom-ins subtle and gradual (ease in and out), never a hard punch.
- Caption highlight colour light sage #B9C8A5, always with a soft shadow behind it.
- Hook title on 2 lines max, above the head, never over the face.
- Captions must land on the word: check caption timing against the cut audio before rendering.
- No background music.
- No added graphics or screenshots beyond captions and a hook title unless asked.
- Never cut a reel before the review.
- Always deliver a cover and a phone-size copy with the full-quality reel.
